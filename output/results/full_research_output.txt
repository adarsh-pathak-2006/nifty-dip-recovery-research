STEP 9: CHALLENGING THE RESULT
======================================================================

--- Potential Sources of Spurious Results ---

1. LOOK-AHEAD BIAS:
   ✓ Mitigated: Entry at next-day Open (unknowable at event close).
   ✓ Events detected using only close-to-close returns known at EOD.

2. SLIPPAGE & TRANSACTION COSTS:
   Applied 0.20% round-trip cost per trade.
   Real-world costs could be higher during volatile periods.

3. SAMPLE SIZE:
   128 qualifying events over the full sample.
   Adequate for basic parametric tests.

4. OVERLAPPING EVENTS:
   Applied 5-day cooldown to reduce overlap.
   Events during sustained crashes may still be correlated.

5. MARKET REGIMES:
   The data spans multiple regimes (2008 GFC, 2020 COVID, etc.).
   Results may be driven by a small number of extreme episodes.

6. DATA QUALITY:
   Yahoo Finance data is not exchange-official.
   Minor discrepancies vs. NSE official data may exist.

7. MULTIPLE TESTING:
   We test multiple holding periods and thresholds.
   Without Bonferroni or similar correction, some may be
   significant by chance alone.

--- Falsification Criteria ---
The hypothesis would be rejected if:
  • Mean forward returns are not significantly > 0 (p > 0.05)
  • Event returns are not meaningfully different from baseline
  • Results do not replicate out-of-sample
  • Net-of-cost backtest returns are negative
  • Results are driven by ≤ 3 extreme events
